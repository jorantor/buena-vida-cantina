Event FAQs

How many guests can Buena Vida accommodate?

Buena Vida can accommodate up to 120 guests for a full buyout. Planning something larger? Our sister venue Lot F is right next door, and the two spaces can be combined to host events of up to approximately 270 guests.

How far is Buena Vida from Moscone Center?

Buena Vida is an 8-minute walk from Moscone Center.

What types of events work best at Buena Vida?

Buena Vida is at its best when the goal is to bring people closer together. The intimate scale works especially well for social events where people can sit, share food, move around, and actually spend time together. Buena Vida really shines when the event is less about watching a stage and more about conversation, connection, and sharing the night with the people around you.

What food options are available?

Private event menus can include taco bars, family-style dinners, passed appetizers, heavy appetizers, buffet service, desserts, and custom group menus.

Can Buena Vida accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes. Please communicate any dietary requirements at least one week before the event.

Can we bring our own entertainment and outside vendors?

Yes. We encourage hosts to make the event their own. You’re welcome to bring in DJs, entertainment, décor, equipment, and outside vendors, as long as everything is coordinated with and approved by the venue in advance.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes. There are several public parking garages in the Moscone / Yerba Buena area, including Moscone Center Garage at 255 Third Street and Fifth & Mission / Yerba Buena Garage at 833 Mission Street.

Is Buena Vida a good fit for conference events near Moscone?

Absolutely. The 8-minute walk from Moscone Center makes Buena Vida especially convenient for post-conference dinners, client events, networking receptions, happy hours, and team celebrations.
